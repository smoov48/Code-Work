History about Monster School

Minecraft Monster School is a popular Minecraft animation genre, originally created by YouTube user Willcraft, featuring Herobrine teaching hostile mobs (Endie, Skellington, Creeper, etc.) subjects like crafting, running, and cooking. Rising to popularity in the early 2010s, it evolved from an original 13-episode series into a widely replicated, often satirical, "genre" or meme featuring various mob student scenarios.
